Understanding Cyber Scams

Cyber scams trick individuals into voluntary financial transfers or handing over confidential information through deception, urgency, and manipulation.

Viruses & Malware Spectrum

Malware (Malicious Software) includes any software intentionally designed to cause damage to a computer, server, client, or computer network.

Ransomware Deep-Dive

Ransomware is a severe malware form that encrypts a victim's files, demanding payment (often cryptocurrency) to regain access.

Identifying Spam & Phishing

Phishing attacks rely on tricking victims into clicking unsafe links or revealing passwords via fake emails, SMS (Smishing), or voice calls (Vishing).
